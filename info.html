About Us & Culinary Heritage

Bolu Kambu (also widely known as Juku Kambu) represents one of the most cherished authentic culinary delicacies from the Bugis-Makassar culture, specifically rooted in Pangkajene and Kepulauan (Pangkep) Regency, South Sulawesi.

Operating since 2016 in Mappasaile Village, Bu Desi Souvenir Shop is dedicated to keeping this ancestral recipe alive. We meticulously process fresh local milkfish by deboning, seasoning the meat with local secret spices, and re-stuffing it back into the intact fish skin before vacuum-sealing or frying.

Product Specifications & Storage

🌱 Ingredients & Seasoning

Made of fresh Pangkep milkfish meat, aromatic turmeric, ground pepper, garlic, shallots, toasted coconut blend, and natural seasonings without artificial preservatives.

❄️ Shelf Life & Preservation

• Room Temperature: Up to 2 days.
• Chilled (Refrigerator): Up to 7 days.
• Frozen (Freezer -18°C): Up to 1-2 months in sealed vacuum packaging.
