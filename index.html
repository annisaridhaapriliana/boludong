Milkfish Filling (Bolu Kambu)

Traditional Heritage Culinary of Pangkajene

Bolu Kambu (also known as Juku Kambu) is a traditional dish originating from the Pangkajene and Kepulauan (Pangkep) Regency in South Sulawesi. This unique dish is made by filleting milkfish to separate the meat from the bones and skin. The meat is cooked with traditional spices—such as turmeric, pepper, garlic, and shallots—and then stuffed back inside the fish skin before being fried or frozen.

Established in 2016, Mrs. Desi's Souvenir Shop in Mappasaile Village is dedicated to preserving this traditional culinary heritage and making it accessible to local residents and visitors as a signature regional souvenir.

This platform is developed as a prototype and digital innovation solution under the Learning Express (LeX) project to improve business productivity, streamline administrative workflows, and expand the market reach of Mrs. Desi's Bolu Kambu products.
